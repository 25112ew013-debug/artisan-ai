Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app really use AI, or is it a mockup?

It really uses AI. Background removal runs a U2Net neural network in your browser. Speech recognition uses the browser's native AI engine. Pricing uses a real cost + market model. No fake responses.

Which languages are supported for voice input?

Hindi, English, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, and Punjabi — depending on your browser. Chrome and Edge have the best support.

Does it cost anything?

No. The entire app runs in your browser. No API keys, no subscriptions, no hidden fees. You can host it free on GitHub Pages.

Is my data safe?

All processing happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Your photos and voice never leave your device.

Why does background removal take 30 seconds the first time?

The AI model (~40MB) downloads once and is cached. Subsequent uses take 2–3 seconds.

Ready to take your craft digital?

Free forever. Works offline. No signup needed.
